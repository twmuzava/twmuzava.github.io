Takudzwa Wilson Muzavazi

My name is Takudzwa Wilson Muzavazi, and I’m a master’s student in Geographic Information Science at Michigan Technological University, graduating in April 2027.

I’m interested in applying GIS, remote sensing, and spatial analysis across environmental management, agriculture, transportation, and infrastructure. My experience includes using Sentinel-2 imagery to study forest health in Zimbabwe, mapping railway infrastructure and derailment hotspots, and analyzing moose distribution on Isle Royale, where I support the Wolf–Moose Project as a research assistant.
