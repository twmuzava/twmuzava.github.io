Summary

GIS & programming: ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, Google Earth Engine, ArcGIS ModelBuilder, Python, R

Spatial analysis: Hotspot and kernel density, proximity and buffer analysis, IDW, Empirical Bayesian Kriging, least-cost path, Bayesian habitat modelling
